## [Unreleased]

### Added — wallet custody: deposit, play and withdraw with a real wallet (devnet; ADR 0005, spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-24-wallet-custody-design.md`)
- **Deposit SOL from your wallet, play it, withdraw it back.** A deposit is an ordinary transfer to the treasury hot wallet, signed in Phantom/Solflare; the API verifies it on chain by the treasury's **balance change** (not by parsing instructions), attributes it to the account owning the sending wallet (a transaction signed by wallets of two accounts is held for a human, never auto-credited), and credits it once (`txSignature` unique). A scan of the treasury's history (job every minute, hourly cron, "check again" button) credits deposits whose confirm call never arrived. Withdrawals go only to a wallet linked to the account, within per-request and daily caps, one at a time; the server signs them from the hot wallet.
- **A withdrawal pays at most once.** Its signature and blockhash expiry are stored *before* broadcast; a new signature is signed only after the previous is provably dead (landed with an error, or unseen once the finalized height passed its expiry), and a refund happens only on that same proof — never on "the send threw" (the pattern behind BACKLOG C4). Tested against an in-memory ledger (drops, failures, expiry, racing processors, max-attempt refund) and against a **real local validator** (`solana-test-validator` in docker compose and CI).
- **Custody only runs on a proven cluster.** Enabled is a setting; active needs the RPC's genesis hash to match `SOLANA_NETWORK`, and mainnet never activates in this phase (it needs a managed signer and the real-money gate) — a mistyped RPC URL cannot move real money.
- **Play-money cannot become withdrawable.** Play-money accounts keep crash and coinflip; while custody is on, jackpot and lottery (one shared pot per round) — and the hidden blackjack, whose seats are in memory between bet and deal — take deposited SOL only, a PvP coinflip is joined only from the creator's balance type, airdrop tips and the daily race stay play-money, referral commission accrues only between accounts of the same type, and the first deposit waits while play-money bets are still running. The first deposit converts the account: the play balance, unclaimed commission and earned free tickets are forfeited in the same transaction.
- **Web:** `/wallet` rebuilt (devnet guide for Phantom/Solflare + faucet, deposit with live status, withdraw, history with explorer links, hold reasons in plain words); a test-network banner; the balance pill says PLAY or SOL; jackpot/lottery tell play accounts why they need a deposit; the coinflip lobby marks flips of the other balance type.
- **Ops:** `CUSTODY_*` settings forwarded to the container; custody solvency in the reconcile job (`scadium_custody_hot_wallet_lamports`, `scadium_custody_liabilities_lamports`); the global pause now also stops new withdrawal signatures. Runbook: `docs/runbooks/cloudflare.md` → Wallet custody.

### Removed
- **The vault-PDA deposit bridge** (`vault-bridge.service.ts`, `/vault/deposit-confirm`, `/vault/withdraw-confirm`, `/vault/balance`, the web vault tx builders). It was never enabled and could not hold value: the owner could withdraw without the server, so losses were uncollectable, while `settle_bet` let the server key move any amount out of any vault (ADR 0005). `VaultTransfer` became `CustodyTransfer`; `User.vaultAddress` was replaced by `User.fundedAt` (not carried over — an old vault's SOL is still in its owner's PDA).

### Fixed
- **The container never received the RPC URL**: it was forwarded as `SOLANA_RPC`, which nothing reads (the API reads `SOLANA_RPC_URL`); the dead `HOUSE_WALLET_SECRET_KEY` forward is gone too.

### Fixed — four-games hardening, Tier A (live bugs; plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-23-four-games-hardening.md`)
- **Lottery drew ~35 times a day instead of once (H8).** Boot recovery settled every `open` draw and overwrote its `drawAt`; on Cloudflare the hourly cron boots the container, so production settled 98 draws in 48h. Recovery now settles only draws whose `drawAt` has passed and **resumes** the one that is not due (tallies rebuilt from its tickets, timer re-armed).
- **Crash: a rejected duplicate bet deleted the accepted bet's row.** A second `POST /crash/bet` in the same round inserted nothing, was rejected, refunded itself — and then deleted the `CrashBet` row by `(roundId, userId)`, which was the accepted bet's. Cash-outs then failed and a restart could not refund the stake. The duplicate is now rejected inside the debit transaction.
- **A failed settle no longer freezes a game.** Crash, jackpot and lottery settles run with an explicit, config-driven transaction budget (was Prisma's 5 s default) and are retried with backoff; after the last retry crash hands the round to boot recovery, the jackpot refunds, the lottery keeps retrying. A watchdog resumes any loop that stops advancing; fire-and-forget loop steps no longer produce unhandled rejections; a stale timer can no longer close the next round early; leader election no longer flickers to "not leader" mid-renew. One dead-letter per round, not per attempt. (`apps/api/src/games/round-loop.ts`)
- **Crash drains on shutdown.** On SIGTERM (the hourly cron stop, the budget stop, a deploy) crash takes no new bets or rounds, refunds queued next-round bets at once, and lets the round in flight bust and settle — instead of voiding it and returning 1× to a player riding 3×. The client now explains a round that ended while it was disconnected, with the real outcome.
- **The lottery settle scales.** Aggregates are folded per user and Bet rows go in with `createMany`; a 1,000-ticket draw settles in ~1 s (it used to be ~7 statements per ticket in one 5 s transaction).
- **Every game resyncs after a reconnect**, shows when it is offline, and ignores events for a round it has not been shown; crash disables cash-out while disconnected and no longer lets a stale snapshot hide a player's new bet (H16 residual).
- **The daily budget now counts socket-held time.** It charges container *running* time from a Durable Object heartbeat and stops the container (gracefully) once the cap is spent; it used to charge request gaps, so one idle tab kept the container up nearly all day while the "1 hour a day" cap saw ~80 s an hour. Crash cash-out stays reachable past the cap.

### Fixed — four-games hardening, Tier B (real-money blockers)
- **Affiliate commission is a share of the house edge, not the stake.** At 5–15% of the stake it was more than coinflip's entire 5% take, so a referred pair flipping against each other out-earned the house on every flip with zero risk. Commission = stake × `GAME_HOUSE_EDGE[game]` × tier rate (the bc.game formula; the lottery keeps no edge, so it earns volume but no commission). Invariant tests: the two-account ring leaves the house positive; no game/tier pays more than stake × edge.
- **Crash pays what it advertises.** Payouts now respect the per-bet net-win cap (`HOUSE.MAX_WIN_PER_BET_LAMPORTS`: a riding position exits automatically where it reaches the cap; every cash-out is clamped — a 100 SOL bet at 1,000× used to pay 100,000 SOL). The published RTP is the formula's real **94.05%** at every target (it said 95%), derived in `CRASH.RTP` and locked by a closed-form test and a 200k-round Monte-Carlo run; the web registry's hand-typed 95% for all twelve games now reads `GAME_RTP`.
- **Jackpot winners are verifiable.** The settle persists every entry's ticket range in a total order (`rangesJson`), each Bet carries its own range, `GET /jackpot/rounds/:id` serves a settled round, and the verifier checks the winning ticket *and* whose range holds it.
- **No operator foreknowledge in crash, jackpot or lottery (ADR 0004).** Results fold in the first **drand** beacon round published after bets / entries / sales close — a public value nobody, the operator included, can know while the round is open. No beacon in time → crash voids and refunds, the jackpot retries then refunds, the lottery retries; never a seed-only fallback. The verifier fetches the beacon value from the public relays itself. The lottery's "slot hash" was synthetic (operator-known) in production while the UI claimed Solana entropy — that copy is gone.
- **Coinflip broadcast its revealed seed before the commit.** Everything is now emitted after the transaction commits. RG limits count unsettled stakes (open flips, tickets in undrawn draws), and a self-exclusion or cooling-off cancels + refunds open flips. Lottery free tickets are redeemed with one guarded UPDATE (20 parallel requests redeemed one ticket 20×).

### Added — four-games hardening, Tier C (playable on a quiet site)
- **Coinflip vs the house**: create a flip that resolves at once against the house at the same 1.9×, or send your own open flip to the house. The outcome derives from the creator's own seed + nonce (they pick the moment, so the operator never selects). Unjoined PvP flips expire after an hour and are refunded; a per-user open-flip cap; `GET /coinflip/mine` and a "your open flips / locked" strip.
- **Lottery bulk buy** in one request at the advertised bulk price (it charged full price per ticket via a loop, and hit the throttle); the slip quotes play money in SOL; quantity capped at a purchase's limit.
- **Jackpot reveal** built from the settle's own ranges, landing on the real ticket; "You won" decided by id (it could tell the biggest staker they won when a last-second entrant did).

### Changed — four-games hardening, Tier D (polish)
- **Crash history** survives restarts (last 50 busted rounds loaded at boot), and every chip opens the verifier for that round; the snapshot no longer serves the oldest 20 rounds.
- **Crash auto-bet** runs on the shared engine: number of bets, on-win/on-loss progression, stop on profit/loss. **Space** bets or cashes out.
- **Mobile coinflip lobby** as cards (Join was off-screen at 375px); a creator sees their flip when it is joined; "Win X" preview; exact decimal SOL parsing everywhere ('0.3' was placed 1 lamport short).
- **No userIds or full wallets on the wire**: crash and jackpot broadcasts and public snapshots carry a display handle and an opaque `publicPlayerId`; `/me` returns the caller's `publicId`.

### Changed
- **Jackpot: the clock starts with the players.** A round waits (no timer, no empty-round churn); the first entry sets a 5-minute solo deadline (then a refund), the second distinct player starts the 45 s countdown. Restarts resume a waiting or counting-down round instead of drawing it during boot. "Recent winners" lists drawn rounds only; an entered player sees "You're in" instead of a button the API rejects. Migration: `JackpotRound.closeAt` nullable.
- **Scope narrowed to four games: Crash, Coinflip, Jackpot, Lottery** (live). Blackjack, Dice, Limbo, Wheel, Plinko, Mines, Hi-Lo and Tower are *hidden, not deleted* — `ENABLED_GAMES` (default: the four) gates them at Nest module registration, so their REST routes 404 and the blackjack Socket.io namespace refuses connections; the web app 404s their pages and drops them from every menu, the landing grid and `sitemap.xml`. Deleting them was rejected: shrinking the Prisma `GameType` enum needs a cast that cannot run while any `Bet` row holds a removed value, so it would mean deleting real bet history (and CI would stay green, because it migrates a fresh DB). Re-enabling a game is one env var. The catalogue now has **one source of truth** (`GAME_CATALOG` in `packages/shared`), replacing ~18 hand-maintained lists that had drifted — the landing page had been missing Jackpot and Lottery entirely. Historical surfaces (bet history, live ticker, fairness verifier) still resolve all twelve so past bets keep their label and stay verifiable. Guarded by `src/games/gating-coverage.spec.ts` and an e2e 404 lock per hidden game.
- **Platform: Railway → Cloudflare (live on `scadium.com`).** The whole stack now runs on Cloudflare: `apps/web` on Workers via `@opennextjs/cloudflare`, and `apps/api` + `apps/worker` as a single **Cloudflare Container** built from the existing Dockerfile, fronted by a Worker + Durable Object. The money core is untouched — no change to `apps/api/src/games/**`, `prisma/**`, the 53 migrations, or the 313-case test suite. Railway's project was deleted; `scadium.com`, `www` and `api.scadium.com` are attached as **zone routes** (the apex already resolved through Cloudflare's proxy, so routes intercept at the edge without needing DNS:Edit to remove the stale Railway records). Verified live: zero Railway headers, all pages 200 in ~80 ms, and a real WebSocket upgrade to `wss://api.scadium.com` receiving `crash:tick` / `crash:bust` / `crash:round-start`.
- **The 9 economy jobs are now driven by Cloudflare Cron Triggers.** Their bodies were inline in the worker's BullMQ consumer array; a second caller would have meant copy-pasting money-moving logic, so they were extracted into one shared registry (`apps/api/src/jobs/job-registry.ts`) that both drivers call. A unit test asserts the registry's key set equals `Object.values(QUEUE_NAMES)`, so a new queue cannot ship without a handler. The cron hits **one generic route** — `POST /api/v1/internal/jobs` — not nine. `apps/worker/src/main.ts` dropped 257 → 184 lines with no behaviour change.
- **The site is capped at ~1 hour of container active time per UTC day** (`DAILY_ACTIVE_SECONDS`, accounted in the container's own Durable Object storage). Past it the API returns 503 with `Retry-After` and the live games are offline until midnight UTC; `/health` stays exempt and the check fails *open*, because it is a cost guard rather than a security control. Combined with the hourly cron calling `stop()` as soon as its sweep finishes, an **unvisited site now costs $0** on top of the $5 Workers Paid plan — previously the cron woke the container and let it idle, burning ~73 container-hours/month (8.8× the plan's included 25 GiB-hours) and 73% of Neon free's 100 CU-hours with nobody on the site.
- **Redis has no third-party vendor.** Cloudflare offers no managed Redis, and Scadium only uses it for ephemeral coordination (SIWS nonces, throttle counters, TTL'd locks, BullMQ), so the container runs `redis-server` on loopback with persistence disabled. The planned Upstash dependency was dropped entirely. Postgres on **Neon** (free tier) is now the only non-Cloudflare dependency in the system — Cloudflare has no Postgres, and D1 was evaluated and disqualified (no interactive transactions, BigInt above 2^53 silently rounds, and 0 of 53 migrations survive a sqlite datasource).

- **"Aurum" visual identity — de-solpump theme redesign:** the first look (deep-purple canvas + magenta→indigo neon) read as a solpump.io clone; replaced it with a warm premium-arcade identity — **deep obsidian canvas + jackpot-gold primary (`#FFBE3D`) + electric-teal accent (`#2DD4BF`)** and a gold→coral signature gradient (`#FFCE5E→#FF7A45`). Recolored the brand (logo + favicon), the landing games-grid (varied jewel tiles), win confetti, and every bespoke game-art surface (crash rocket/curve, wheel, plinko, blackjack, jackpot, 3D card scenes) — pure colour-constant swaps, **no game logic touched**. Verified: **0** magenta/indigo occurrences remain in the deployed CSS. `docs/design-system.md` rewritten as the authoritative Aurum spec + a reusable master design prompt.

### Fixed
- **Container cold start never opened its port in time.** `@cloudflare/containers` waits 20 s for the container to listen; measured boot is 36 s at the `basic` instance's 0.25 vCPU (redis ~1 s, `prisma migrate deploy` ~8 s, NestJS ~14 s+), so every cold start failed with *"the container is not listening in the TCP address"*. The BullMQ worker now waits for the API's `/health` before starting, instead of building its own Nest graph concurrently and starving the API of CPU. An attempt to instead hold requests open with a 180 s `startAndWaitForPorts()` was reverted — it hung every request for three minutes and wedged the Durable Object — as was a rethrowing `onError`, which runs on the container monitor and prevented restarts. Sub-20 s is not reachable at any instance size while migrations run on boot, so the accepted behaviour is: first request after an idle period fails, the container boots in the background, and everything from ~40 s on serves in ~1 s (socket.io reconnects on its own). Documented in `docs/runbooks/cloudflare.md`.
- **The container could never restart after stopping.** `max_instances: 1` meant a single slot, and stopped instances stay *registered* as `inactive` — including a stale one from an earlier `CONTAINER_INSTANCE` name. With two registered against a cap of one, nothing could be scheduled and every request failed with *"the container is not running, consider calling start()"*. Raised to 3. It is headroom, not replicas: the Worker addresses one Durable Object by name, so exactly one container serves and the H12 single-replica constraint is enforced by DO identity.
- **API image was 3.65 GB against a 4 GB disk.** The unfiltered workspace install pulled `apps/web`'s tree into the API image — `next`, `workerd`, `@next/swc`, six copies of `viem`, `three.js`, `lucide-react` ×2, `react-aria` — none of it reachable from the API. Filtering the install to `@scadium/api...` and `@scadium/worker...` cut it to **1.39 GB**.
- **`apps/web/.env.production` was gitignored**, so a fresh clone would have built the Workers bundle against `localhost` instead of `api.scadium.com`. Every key in it is `NEXT_PUBLIC_*` (inlined into the client bundle, public by definition), so it is now versioned with an explicit `.gitignore` negation and a comment forbidding non-public values.
- **Airdrop tip modal "layout disappears" (UI defect):** the `Dialog` rendered `fixed inset-0 z-[100]` but was opened from the `position: sticky` left rail, whose stacking context trapped the overlay so the crash SVG (`z-[5]`) painted over it and the modal vanished. `Dialog` now renders through a portal into `document.body` (SSR-safe mount via `useSyncExternalStore`, no setState-in-effect), so its fixed/z-index positioning is viewport-relative. Verified in production: the modal renders centred on a dimmed backdrop with the element at its centre inside the dialog. `apps/web/src/components/ui/dialog.tsx`.
- **Crash betting-window countdown desync on page refresh:** the waiting-phase countdown always seeded to a fresh full 15s whenever it saw `phase === 'waiting'`, so a mid-window page refresh restarted from 15 instead of the true remaining time (read as "counts down from 15 over and over on refresh"). The snapshot now carries `bettingMsRemaining` — the window's remaining time as a RELATIVE value, which the client anchors to its own clock (skew-proof) — and the countdown counts to that anchored end. `crash.engine.ts` (new `waitingStartedAt` round-state + `bettingMsRemaining` in the snapshot), `use-crash.ts` (`bettingMsRemaining` in state + round-start), `crash-curve.tsx` (anchored countdown). Test: `crash-betting-countdown.e2e-spec.ts` (snapshot reports remaining while waiting, null once running).
- **Crash schedule→cancel affiliate-commission mint (money bug, found by the pre-merge audit):** crash credited referral commission at SCHEDULE time, but a scheduled next-round bet is refundable via `cancelScheduled` — so a `schedule → cancel` loop accrued commission at zero net cost, and the new `#H18 claim()` turned that stranded commission into spendable play balance (balance minted from nothing). Fix: the referrer is credited only when the scheduled stake **drains into a live round** (`crash.engine.startNewRound`, atomic with the CrashBet-create + ScheduledCrashBet-delete tx — the irrevocable-commit point), never at schedule time; a cancelled schedule now earns nothing. Mirrors the immediate `placeBet` at-commit credit and the other games' settlement-time credit. `apps/api/src/games/crash/crash.service.ts` (removed the schedule-time credit), `crash.engine.ts` (credit at drain, `@Optional AffiliatesService`). Test: `crash-schedule-referral.e2e-spec.ts` (schedule→cancel→claim mints nothing; drain credits) — red→green.
- **Worker ships in the single-VPS prod compose (H19):** `docker-compose.prod.yml` now runs a `worker` service (postgres/redis/api/web/caddy previously had no worker), so the 9 BullMQ queues — dividends, block mining, vault accrual, airdrop, reconciliation/solvency, reward + lottery payouts, leaderboard, burn — actually run on that deploy path. The Helm chart (real prod path) already shipped `worker.yaml`; this closes the compose gap.
- **Public `/metrics` token gate:** the Prometheus scrape endpoint now accepts an optional `METRICS_TOKEN` — when set it requires `Authorization: Bearer <token>` (or `?token=`, timing-safe compare); when unset it stays open (backward-compatible). The edge proxy (Caddy/Railway) forwards every path to the API, so `/metrics` was reachable on the public origin and network isolation alone didn't cover it. Belt-and-suspenders with the documented Cloudflare WAF path rule. `apps/api/src/observability/metrics.controller.ts`. Test: `metrics.controller.spec.ts`.
- **Affiliate coverage across all games (#47, money bug):** referral commission used to accrue only on crash + coinflip — referred play on the other 10 games earned referrers nothing. `creditReferral` now fires in every settlement transaction (the shared instant-settle + stateful-round paths, and the blackjack/jackpot/lottery engines), alongside `proofOfWager.accrue`. New `referral-coverage.spec.ts` source-scan guard maps every `GameType` to its crediting module (so a new game can't ship outside the affiliate program), plus an end-to-end `affiliate-settle.e2e-spec.ts` case proving a dice bet credits the referrer.
- **On-chain forged-event hardening (H5/H5a):** `verifyVaultTransfer` and `verifyTicketTx` now trust only `Program data:` events emitted while the configured program (vault / lottery) is the active invoke frame — a new `programScopedEventPayloads` walks the Solana log invoke stack (CPI-aware), so a look-alike program in the same transaction can no longer forge a Deposited/Withdrawn or TicketBought event. Gates real-money vault deposits + on-chain lottery tickets. `apps/api/src/solana/vault-events.ts`, `chain.service.ts`. Test: `vault-events.spec.ts` (forged-emitter + CPI-leak rejection).
- **Web hardening:** locked `images.remotePatterns` from wildcard `**` to `[]` (nothing uses next/image with remote hosts — the wildcard was an open, paid image proxy); added `robots.ts` + `sitemap.ts` (SEO scaffolding, scadium.com); tightened avatar `avatarUrl` validation to base64 raster images / http(s) / empty only, rejecting SVG and other script-capable data URLs (stored-XSS vector). Tests: `update-profile-avatar.spec.ts`.
- **Graceful shutdown:** `main.ts` now calls `app.enableShutdownHooks()`, so SIGTERM/SIGINT (every Railway/PaaS rolling deploy) runs the modules' `OnModuleDestroy` hooks — the crash/jackpot/lottery/blackjack engines release leadership and clear their loops, BullMQ stops, Prisma/Redis close — instead of an abrupt mid-round kill.
- **Crash client reconnect-resync (H16):** the crash hook now re-fetches `/crash/snapshot` on every socket (re)connect and authoritatively resyncs round state, so a WebSocket drop across the waiting→running transition (a rolling deploy / edge reset) can no longer leave `phase` stuck at 'waiting' and disable the cash-out button on a live bet. `apps/web/src/hooks/use-crash.ts`.
- **Log-redaction: geo proxy secret (H15):** `x-geo-proxy-secret` (the trusted-proxy credential that authenticates geo headers) is now in the pino redaction list, so it can't leak from logs and be replayed to defeat geoblocking. Test: `redaction.spec.ts`.
- **List-endpoint limit hardening:** a shared `parseLimit()` normalizes every `?limit` query param (NaN/blank→fallback, fractional truncated, clamped to [1,max]) across all 16 list controllers, so a `?limit=abc`/`?limit=1.5` can no longer reach Prisma's `take` as an invalid value. `apps/api/src/common/parse-limit.ts`.
- **Lottery play-money payout (H4):** in the default chain-disabled mode the settle now credits each winning ticket's lamport prize to the winner's play balance (`applyBalanceDelta`, ledgered). Previously tickets debited the play balance but every prize path no-op'd unless the on-chain lottery was enabled, so play-money winners were never paid. On-chain mode still pays $SCAD on-chain (gated, no double-pay). `apps/api/src/games/lottery/lottery.engine.ts`. Test: `apps/api/test/lottery-playmoney-payout.e2e-spec.ts`.

Also fixed the `metadataBase` and affiliate referral URL from scadium.io → scadium.com.
- **Affiliate commission claim (H18):** accrued affiliate commission is now collectible — `POST /affiliates/claim` credits `(commission - claimed)` across the referrer's non-flagged referrals into their play balance under a Serializable transaction, advancing a new `commissionClaimedLamports` marker so each lamport pays once. Stats expose `claimableCommissionLamports`/`claimedCommissionLamports`. Migration `20260716120000_affiliate_commission_claimed`. Test: `apps/api/test/affiliate-claim.e2e-spec.ts`.
- **Hourly settle cadence (H11):** airdrop pool, staker dividends, block mining, and vault accrual now settle the last COMPLETED clock hour (`lastCompletedHourPeriod`) instead of the in-progress one. The worker fires every ~5 minutes, so previously each ran on only the first minutes of the current hour (and the airdrop then rejected tips for the rest of the hour). New shared helper in `queue.constants.ts`; applied to `airdrop.engine`, `airdrop.service`, `distribution.service`, `block-mining.service`, `vault-accrual.service`. Test: `queue.constants.spec.ts` + updated hourly e2e suites.
- **Blackjack betting-window restart (H1):** accepted bets are now persisted to the round's `stateJson` as they are placed/cleared during the betting window (`persistBettingStakes`), so a restart mid-window refunds them via boot recovery instead of finding an empty `{}` and confiscating the debited stakes. `placeBet`/`clearBet`/`leaveSeat` became async. `apps/api/src/games/blackjack/blackjack.engine.ts`. Test: `apps/api/test/blackjack-betting-recovery.e2e-spec.ts`.
- **Responsible-gambling limit bypass (H20):** coinflip `join` now passes the flip's stake and lottery `buyTicket` passes the ticket's lamport cost to `assertCanWager` (both previously passed `0n`, which short-circuits the daily wager/loss-limit check). A self-limited player can no longer bypass their limit by joining flips or buying tickets. `coinflip.service.ts`, `lottery.service.ts`. Tests: `coinflip.service.spec.ts`, `lottery-rg.spec.ts`.
- **Crash auto-cashout below bust (H3):** an auto-cashout whose target is below the committed bust point is now paid at that target regardless of 20 Hz tick granularity (previously a tick that jumped past bust made `cashOut` re-derive the live multiplier, see it >= bust, and throw — voiding the win). Payout also rounds instead of flooring (`Math.floor(m*100)` underpaid 0.01×). `apps/api/src/games/crash/crash.engine.ts`. Test: `apps/api/test/crash-autocashout-target.e2e-spec.ts`.
- **Seed-rotation fairness (C3):** `SeedManagerService.rotateServerSeed` now refuses (Serializable + active-round check) while any stateful round (mines/tower/hilo) is in progress, so a player can no longer reveal the active server seed mid-round to reproduce the committed layout and win deterministically. `apps/api/src/fairness/seed-manager.service.ts`. Test: `apps/api/test/seed-rotation.e2e-spec.ts`.
- **Coinflip cancel/join TOCTOU (H2):** `cancel()` now claims the flip with a guarded compare-and-swap (`updateMany where status='open'`) before refunding, mirroring `join()`. A cancel racing a concurrent join can no longer both refund the creator's stake and let the flip resolve (money duplication). `apps/api/src/games/coinflip/coinflip.service.ts`. Regression: `coinflip.service.spec.ts` cancel-CAS unit guard.
- **Blackjack money-integrity (C1/C2):** a rebet placed during the 5-second "settled" result pause no longer refunds the already-settled stake (money creation), and other seats' prior-round bets no longer ride the next round without a fresh debit. `apps/api/src/games/blackjack/blackjack.engine.ts` now clears every seat's bet when a new bet opens a round from the `settled` phase. ### Added
- **Auto-bet for the instant games (#roadmap-6, retention):** dice/limbo/plinko/wheel now have a Manual/Auto toggle with a full auto-bet engine — number of bets (0 = until stopped), on-win / on-loss stake progression (reset or increase-by-%, i.e. martingale), and stop-on-profit / stop-on-loss — the category staple that previously existed only for crash (and crash can't do outcome-driven logic since its settlement is async). A shared game-agnostic `useAutoBet` hook drives the loop: because the instant `/play` returns the settled result synchronously, it `await`s each bet one at a time, reads the outcome, applies progression (clamped to the game's min/max), and honours the stop conditions; ANY bet error (server RG/balance/throttle rejection) stops it, and unmount halts it. It cannot bypass any server control — every iteration hits the same `/play` that enforces RG + the guarded balance debit per bet. Paced at `AUTOBET.BET_INTERVAL_MS` (500 ms) to stay under the per-route bet throttle. New: `use-auto-bet.ts`, `auto-bet-controls.tsx`, `bet-mode-tabs.tsx`, `AUTOBET` in `@scadium/shared`. Test: `use-auto-bet.test.tsx` (count, martingale, reset-on-win, max-clamp, stop-on-profit/loss, error-stop).
- **Daily race + windowed leaderboards (#roadmap-5, retention):** the hourly `LeaderboardSnapshot` was being written and never read; the boards were all-time-only. Added **daily (UTC day)** and **weekly (ISO week)** windowed boards ranked by wagered volume (`GET /leaderboard/window?period=`), plus a **daily race**: today's top wagerers split a fixed play-money pool (`RACE` in `@scadium/shared`, 50 SOL over a top-10 bps curve summing to 100%), settled when the UTC day completes. `GET /leaderboard/race` returns live standings + per-rank prize + the pool + the UTC-midnight reset. The worker settles the last COMPLETED day on the hourly leaderboard cadence, Redis-locked and **idempotent** — the new `RaceResult` row (unique `[raceDay, userId]`) is the guarded claim before each prize credit, so a re-run or a concurrent replica pays each winner at most once, all via `applyBalanceDelta` (ledgered). Web: the leaderboard page gains All-time / Today / This-week tabs and a `DailyRaceCard` (standings, prize pool, countdown, your position). Migration `20260716190000_race_result`. Tests: `leaderboard-race.e2e-spec.ts` (settle pays top-N once, excludes banned, cross-run idempotent; windowed ranking ignores out-of-window bets), `packages/fair/src/race.test.ts` (prize curve).
- **Sitewide live-bet feed (#roadmap-4, social proof):** a cross-game settlement broadcast + a thin `LiveBetTicker` strip under the header, the single biggest social-proof surface Scadium lacked vs solpump and every category peer. New `@Global` `LiveModule`: a `/live` Socket.io namespace (`LiveFeedGateway`) firehoses a PII-safe `live:bet` event (display handle — username or shortened wallet, never the full address or userId — plus game/amount/payout/multiplier/won) on every settlement; `LiveFeedService` resolves the display (cached), keeps a small ring, and serves `GET /api/v1/live/bets?limit=&wins=` (DB-backed, durable) as the ticker's initial seed. Every one of the 12 games publishes: the two shared instant/stateful helpers (dice/limbo/wheel/plinko + mines/hilo/tower) and the crash/coinflip/jackpot/lottery/blackjack engines call `publishSettledBet` **post-commit, fire-and-forget** (a feed failure can never touch money; injection is `@Optional` so unit construction is unaffected; lottery/jackpot build their feed list retry-safely). Web: `useLiveFeed` seeds from REST then prepends socket events (deduped by bet id), mounted in the app shell. - **Published per-game RTP on every game page (#roadmap-2, trust):** a single `GAME_RTP` map in `@scadium/shared` derives each game's advertised return-to-player from the SAME `HOUSE_EDGE` constant the payout math uses (`rtpPct(1 - edge)`), so the shown figure can't drift from what players are actually paid — solpump's edge-vs-advertised discrepancy is a named trust failure we avoid by construction.
